Bio:

Park Ji-min also known as Jimin, is a South Korean singer, songwriter/composer, and dancer. He is a member of the worldwide popular boy band, Bangtan Sonyeondan (BTS). Jimin is the lead vocalist and main dancer of BTS. In March 24th, 2023 Jimin released his first solo album 'FACE' with a total of 6 tracks.
